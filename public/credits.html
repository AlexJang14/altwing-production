Software and retained notices

React and React DOM use MIT notices. Lucide icons use ISC, with MIT notices for Feather-derived icons. The Sites Vite plugin retains its original OpenAI MIT notice. Other libraries retain their individual copyright and license texts in the downloadable notice collection.

The locked dependency inventory includes development tools and optional operating-system packages that may never reach the website. Each production build also records its actually bundled packages and corresponding license texts. License metadata alone does not prove compliance or resolve missing notices.

Some toolchain dependencies declare MPL-2.0, LGPL-3.0-or-later or CC-BY-4.0. Whether their source or binary is actually distributed determines additional obligations; they do not grant a license to AltWing’s proprietary code. Any unresolved distribution or source obligation blocks release.

Images, branding and fonts

The active AltWing penguin was extracted with ChatGPT assistance from an uploaded AltWing_logo.svg containing an embedded PNG, according to the founder’s development records. Its matching earlier Git blob and commit document custody, not original copyright ownership. The original creator, generator/account, source and reuse terms remain unresolved. It is not marked rights-cleared.

The inherited hero.png had no source, dynamic-loading or emitted-build references and has been removed from the active source tree. Its hash and provenance remain in the development rights record and version history.

Unused React/Vite starter SVGs and a social-logo sprite are inventoried separately. The favicon, social sprite and React/Vite starter SVGs match Vite’s upstream create-vite template byte-for-byte. Its MIT notice is retained; those logos identify their respective projects and do not imply affiliation.

The application uses system-font fallbacks; it does not download a custom font. No active music, audio, video, remote image or embedded third-party media was identified in this source audit. Toolchain font files are assessed separately from student-facing assets.
